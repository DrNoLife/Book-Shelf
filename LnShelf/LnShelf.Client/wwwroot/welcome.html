A nightly sync checks MangaUpdates, AniList, Apple Books and Open Library, and flags series with a new volume.

Bring your history

How it works

  1. 1

    Add a series

    Search by title. The light novel and the manga of the same story are separate series, so you track the one you actually read.

  2. 2

    Mark your shelf

    Mark what you've read and what you own. The keyboard works on the volume grid, so a long series is quick to catch up.

  3. 3

    Let it keep up

    Come back to see what's next to read, what's new, and what you're behind on buying.
