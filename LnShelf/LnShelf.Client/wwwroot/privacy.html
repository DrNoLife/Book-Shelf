Privacy

LN Shelf is a hobby project that keeps track of your light novel and manga collection.

What is stored

  • Your Google account's email address and its account id, so you can sign in again.
  • The series and volumes you add or import, and what you record about them: read and owned status, dates, prices, notes and covers you upload.
  • When you last signed in.

What is not

  • No password: signing in goes through Google, and no Google token is kept.
  • No tracking, no analytics and no ads. Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.
  • Other users cannot see anything of yours.

Series details and covers come from public catalogues (AniList, MangaUpdates, Open Library and Apple Books). Only titles and ISBNs are sent to them, never anything about you.

Deleting your data

This deletes your account and everything on your shelf, straight away and for good.
